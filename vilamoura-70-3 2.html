Coach notes — read me first ›

The one rule: easy is slower than it feels. Your LT1 is ~6.1 km/h, so most running should feel almost too gentle. Train easy days by heart rate, not pace — and watch your easy pace quicken at the same HR over the months. That's the whole game.

Your engine's good; the endurance floor is the project. Solid VO₂max, cracking power — but low lactate thresholds. Eight months of mostly-easy volume is exactly the fix, so don't rush the easy stuff.

Lifting stays. Keep upper-body hypertrophy fully (Mon chest/arms, Thu shoulders/back). Legs become one lower day of strength + plyometrics — heavier, low volume, plus calf/Achilles and single-leg work to injury-proof the run. Kept off your long ride/run days on purpose.

Swim is your weapon. Surfer + lifeguard = a cold Atlantic in a wetsuit is home turf. Early-April Algarve water sits ~16°C, so a wetsuit is near-certain to be legal — you'll get your buoyancy.

Fuelling (from the Build phase): practise ~60 g carbs per hour on long rides and runs, and drink to thirst. Race-day nutrition is a trained skill.

Bike: mountain bike builds the engine now; get time on the borrowed road bike over summer, and do winter bike sessions on the gym bike / spin. Sort an actual road bike to race on.
