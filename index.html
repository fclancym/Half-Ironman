Coach notes — read me first ›

Two speeds, two rules. Easy days go by heart rate (keep them under 119, slower than feels natural). Hard days go by pace and feel, not HR — heart rate lags 30–60s behind effort, so it's useless for intervals. Every session is tagged Easy Steady Hard Race pace so you know which rule applies.

Easy< 119
Moderate119–155
Hard> 155

~80% easy, ~20% hard. The easy volume is the fix for your low lactate thresholds — bank it and your easy pace quickens on its own. Your easy runs stay easy the whole plan on purpose — the runs get tougher three ways: the long run stretches out (25 min → ~1h45), a weekly tempo comes in during Build, then race-pace runs and bricks arrive in Peak. Lifting stays: upper body full (Mon/Thu), one legs+plyo day kept off long ride/run days. Fuel the work — eat more, not less. Swim edge: surfer/lifeguard, a cold wetsuit Atlantic is home turf.
